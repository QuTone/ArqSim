## Benchmark catalog

The workload catalog is mixed-source; it must not be described as entirely
QASMBench. Exact source-layer hashes and paths are recorded in the source
repository's `server/benchmark/README.md`.

- Seven source-layer circuits are unmodified QASMBench files: `adder_n64`,
  `adder_n118`, `ising_n34`, `ising_n66`, `ising_n98`, `multiplier_n45`, and
  `multiplier_n75`.
- `qft_n29` is an upstream QASMBench
  `*_transpiled.qasm` artifact, despite residing in the local
  `original-circuit/` directory.
- `qaoa_30`, `qft_n15`, `vqe_su2_24`, and `vqe_two_local_24` were generated by
  MQT Bench 2.0.1 with Qiskit 2.1.2, as recorded in their file headers.
- Files under `clifford+T/` and `pbc/` are local derivatives of the
  corresponding source-layer family. They are not upstream benchmark
  artifacts. The source family's upstream license continues to apply.
- `arqsim_timeline_demo` and its normalized derivative are ArqSim-owned.
